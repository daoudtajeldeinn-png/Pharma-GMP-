# IPQC Module 3.1: Capsule Manufacturing

## Overview

Capsule manufacturing requires specific In-Process Quality Control measures to ensure product quality and patient safety.

## Manufacturing Process

- Capsule shell preparation
- Powder blending
- Filling operations
- Sealing and banding
- Polishing and inspection
- Packaging

## IPQC Control Points

- Capsule shell quality
- Powder flow properties
- Fill weight accuracy
- Content uniformity
- Shell integrity
- Moisture content

## Critical Parameters

- Fill weight variation
- Powder density
- Particle size
- Moisture levels
- Machine speed
- Temperature control

## Quality Considerations

- Shell defects
- Fill uniformity
- Content accuracy
- Dissolution properties
- Stability factors
