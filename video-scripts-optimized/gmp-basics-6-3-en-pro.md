## Overview

Managing complaints and recalls is essential to protect patients and company reputation.

What Are Complaints?

A complaint is any contact from patients, doctors, or pharmacists reporting a problem with the product.

Any contact reporting a problem May be from patients, doctors, or pharmacists May be about quality, safety, or efficacy Must be addressed quickly Types of Complaints: Quality Complaints: Product quality problems Safety Complaints: Product safety problems Efficacy Complaints: Product efficacy problems Other Complaints: Packaging or labeling problems Complaints must be recorded immediately Complaints must be investigated Complaints must be addressed quickly Complaint Management Principles  Complaint management must follow specific principles.
