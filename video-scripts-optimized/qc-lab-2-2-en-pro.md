## Overview

Sample management is essential to ensure result accuracy.

Sample Management Principles  Sample management must follow specific principles.

- **Traceability**: Track all samples Storage: Store samples correctly Preservation: Preserve samples from damage Safety: Ensure sample safety Documentation: Document all activities Samples must be tracked from receipt to disposal Samples must be stored in appropriate conditions Samples must be preserved from damage Samples must be handled safely All activities must be documented Sample Receipt Procedures  Sample receipt must follow specific procedures.
- **Receipt**: Receive samples from sender Verify sender identity Verify sample integrity Verify sample quantity Registration: Register samples in log Assign unique sample number Record receipt date Record sample information Inspection: Inspect samples for integrity Inspect samples for quantity Inspect samples for quality Inspect samples for packaging Acceptance or Rejection: Accept samples if suitable Reject samples if not suitable Notify sender of decision Document decision Sample Storage Procedures  Sample storage must follow specific procedures.
- **Initial Storage**: Store samples in appropriate location Store samples in appropriate conditions Store samples by type Store samples by sensitivity Long-term Storage: Store samples for specified period Store samples in appropriate conditions Monitor samples regularly Determine expiration date Special Storage: Store special samples separately Store hazardous samples separately Store sensitive samples separately Store perishable samples separately Protection: Protect samples from damage Protect samples from contamination Protect samples from heat Protect samples from humidity Analysis Procedures  Sample analysis must follow specific procedures.
