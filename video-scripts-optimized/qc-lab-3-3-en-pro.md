## Overview

Accreditation is essential to ensure laboratory competence and reliability.

What is Accreditation?

Accreditation is the official recognition of laboratory competence.

Official recognition of laboratory competence Official recognition of laboratory's ability to produce accurate results Official recognition of laboratory's compliance with standards

- Essential for trust in results of Accreditation:
- Ensures laboratory competence
- Ensures result reliability
- Essential for trust
- Essential for working with clients Accreditation is granted by accredited bodies Accreditation requires comprehensive evaluation Accreditation requires periodic review Accreditation requires continuous improvement Accreditation Bodies  There are several accreditation bodies in different regions.
- **Main Bodies**: ISO/IEC 17025: International standard for laboratories Defines competence requirements Defines quality requirements
- Essential for international accreditation National Accreditation Bodies: Accreditation bodies in each country Grant accreditation to laboratories Review laboratories periodically Monitor performance Regional Accreditation Bodies: Regional accreditation bodies Grant accreditation to laboratories in the region Review laboratories periodically Monitor performance International Accreditation Bodies: International accreditation bodies Grant accreditation to laboratories globally Review laboratories periodically Monitor performance Procedures for Obtaining Accreditation  Obtaining accreditation requires following specific procedures.
- **Application**: Apply for accreditation Submit required documents Pay fees Define accreditation scope Initial Evaluation: Review documents Review system Review procedures Identify improvement areas On-site Evaluation: Visit laboratory Review facilities Review equipment Review personnel Testing: Conduct proficiency tests Conduct calibration tests Conduct comparison tests Evaluate results Granting Accreditation: Grant accreditation if laboratory is qualified Grant accreditation certificate Define accreditation scope Define accreditation period Maintaining Accreditation  Maintaining accreditation is essential for continuity.
