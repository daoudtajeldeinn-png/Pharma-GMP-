# IPQC Module 2.1 Tablet Manufacturing

## Overview

Tablet manufacturing involves multiple stages where IPQC is critical for quality.

## Manufacturing Stages

- Weighing and dispensing
- Granulation and drying
- Blending and mixing
- Compression and tableting
- Coating and polishing

## IPQC Control Points

- Raw material quality
- Granule properties
- Blend uniformity
- Tablet hardness
- Weight variation

## Critical Parameters

- Moisture content
- Particle size distribution
- Bulk density
- Flow properties

## Quality Assurance

- Process validation
- Equipment qualification
- Environmental controls
