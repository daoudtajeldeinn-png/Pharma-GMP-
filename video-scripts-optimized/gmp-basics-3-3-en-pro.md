# GMP Basics - Unit 3 Behavior in Clean Areas  In this lecture, we will discuss behavior in clean areas.

> **Pharmaceutical Quality & Compliance Training Script**

## Overview

Proper behavior is essential to prevent contamination and ensure product quality.

Principles of Behavior in Clean Areas  Clean areas require special behavior to maintain cleanliness and prevent contamination.

- **Minimize Movement**: Excessive movement increases contamination Minimize Talking: Talking releases particles into the air Avoid Touching: Direct touch transfers bacteria Follow Adherence to all procedures Report Problems: Report any deviations Rapid movement increases air currents Loud talking releases more particles Direct touch of product or equipment transfers contamination Procedures exist for a reason Entering Clean Areas  Clean area entry procedures are essential to prevent introducing contamination.
- **Entry Grey Room**: Changing room White Room: Transition area Clean Room: Manufacturing area Detailed Steps: In Grey Room: Remove outer clothing Don clean workwear Wash hands Don gloves In White Room: Don head cover Don face mask Don gown Verify all clothing is worn In Clean Room: Enter slowly Avoid rapid movements Stay in designated path Behavior During Work  Proper behavior during work is essential to prevent contamination.
- **Correct Behaviors**: Slow Movement: Avoid rapid and sudden movements Minimize Talking: Talk only when necessary Avoid Touching: Do not touch face or hair Proper Breathing: Avoid breathing directly on product Use Tools: Use appropriate tools Incorrect Behaviors: Rapid movement Loud talking Touching face or hair Sneezing or coughing without covering Using mobile phone Rapid movement increases air currents Talking releases particles into the air Touching face transfers bacteria to hands Mobile phone is a contamination source Emergency Procedures  Emergency procedures are essential for handling unexpected situations.
