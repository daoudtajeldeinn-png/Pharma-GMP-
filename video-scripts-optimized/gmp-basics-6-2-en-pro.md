## Overview

What is a Deviation?

A deviation is any departure from established procedures or required standards.

Any departure from SOPs Any departure from specifications Any departure from standards Any compliance failure Types of Deviations: Minor Deviations: Small deviations that do not affect quality Major Deviations: Large deviations that may affect quality Critical Deviations: Dangerous deviations that affect patient safety Deviations must be reported immediately Deviations must be documented Deviations must be investigated Deviation Investigation Principles  Deviation investigation must follow specific principles.
