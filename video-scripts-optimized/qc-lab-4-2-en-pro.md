## Overview

Risk management is essential to prevent problems and ensure quality.

What is Risk Management?

Risk management is the process of identifying, evaluating, and managing potential risks.

Identify potential risks Evaluate probability and severity Develop control procedures Monitor risks continuously of Risk Management:

- Prevents potential problems
- Reduces damages
- Improves quality
- Essential for compliance Risk management must be comprehensive Risk management must be continuous Risk management must be documented Risk management must be followed up Types of Risks in Laboratory  There are several types of risks in the laboratory.
- **Quality Risks**: Inaccurate results Unreliable results Inconsistent results Unacceptable results Safety Risks: Employee injuries Laboratory damage Fires Explosions Compliance Risks: Non-compliance with regulations Fines Loss of accreditation Loss of trust Operational Risks: Errors in operations Delays in operations Failures in operations Recurring problems Equipment Risks: Equipment failure Incorrect calibration Insufficient maintenance Incorrect use Risk Management Procedures  Risk management requires following specific procedures.
- **Identify Risks**: Identify all potential risks Identify risk sources Identify risk impact Identify risk probability Evaluate Risks: Evaluate probability of occurrence Evaluate severity of impact Determine risk level Determine priorities Address Risks: Develop control procedures Develop preventive procedures Develop response procedures Develop emergency plans Monitor Risks: Monitor risks continuously Monitor procedure effectiveness Monitor changes in risks Update evaluations Risk Management Tools  There are several tools that can be used for risk management.
