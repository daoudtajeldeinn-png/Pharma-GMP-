## Overview

Defining roles and responsibilities is essential for the success of the system.

of Defining Roles and Responsibilities  Defining roles and responsibilities is essential for several reasons.

- **Main Reasons**: Clarity:
- Ensures each person knows their role Responsibility:
- Ensures each person is responsible for the quality of their work Accountability: Facilitates accountability when problems arise Efficiency: Increases efficiency and effectiveness Improvement: Facilitates performance improvement Roles and responsibilities must be clear Roles and responsibilities must be documented Roles and responsibilities must be current Roles and responsibilities must be available Main Roles  There are several main roles in the quality management system.
- **Role 1**: Top Management Responsibilities: Define quality policy Provide resources Review quality management system Encourage continuous improvement Commitment to quality Role 2: Top Management Representative Responsibilities: Coordinate between top management and quality system Monitor quality system performance Report performance to top management Coordinate internal audit Coordinate management review Role 3: Quality Manager Responsibilities: Manage daily quality management system Develop quality procedures Monitor compliance Manage internal audit Manage continuous improvement Role 4: Department Managers Responsibilities: Implement quality management system in their departments Train employees on quality Monitor quality performance Address problems Improve processes Role 5: Employees Responsibilities: Follow quality procedures Participate in improvement Report problems Continuous training Commitment to quality Documenting Roles and Responsibilities  Documenting roles and responsibilities is essential for compliance.
Content of Roles and Responsibilities Document: Description of each role Description of responsibilities of each role Description of authorities of each role Description of relationships between roles Signature of approval Document must be clear Document must be current Document must be available Document must be traceable Reviewing and Updating Roles and Responsibilities  Roles and responsibilities must be reviewed and updated regularly.

When to Review Roles and Responsibilities?

During organizational changes During system changes During problems During improvements Periodically (annually) How to Review Roles and Responsibilities?
