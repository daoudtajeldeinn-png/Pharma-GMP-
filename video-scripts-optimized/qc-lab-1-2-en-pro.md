# QC Lab & ISO/IEC 17025 - Unit 2 Laboratory Management  In this lecture, we will discuss laboratory management.

> **Pharmaceutical Quality & Compliance Training Script**

## Overview

Effective management is essential for laboratory success.

Laboratory Management Principles  Laboratory management must follow specific principles.

- **Leadership**: Effective leadership is essential Planning: Comprehensive planning is essential Organization: Good organization is essential Communication: Effective communication is essential Improvement: Continuous improvement is essential Management determines organizational culture Management determines direction Management provides resources Management encourages improvement Laboratory Structure  Laboratory structure must be properly organized.
- **Main Departments**: Receiving Department: Receive samples Register samples Inspect samples Store samples Analysis Department: Perform analyses Use equipment Develop methods Monitor quality Quality Department: Monitor quality Internal audit Document control CAPA management Administration Department: Employee management Resource management Budget management Relationship management Maintenance Department: Equipment maintenance Equipment calibration Equipment repair Equipment replacement Personnel Management  Personnel management is essential for laboratory success.
- **Hiring**: Hire qualified employees Hire experienced employees Hire skilled employees Hire competent employees Training: Training on analytical methods Training on equipment Training on quality management Training on safety Evaluation: Evaluate performance regularly Evaluate competence regularly Evaluate skills regularly Evaluate behavior regularly Development: Develop skills Develop competence Develop knowledge Develop experience Equipment Management  Equipment management is essential for laboratory success.
