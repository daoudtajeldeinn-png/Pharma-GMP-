## Overview

Internal audit is essential to ensure compliance and quality.

What is Internal Audit?

Internal audit is a systematic and independent examination of laboratory activities.

Systematic examination of activities Independent examination of activities Compliance evaluation Effectiveness evaluation of Internal Audit:

- Ensures compliance with standards
- Ensures quality of results Identifies improvement areas
- Essential for accreditation Internal audit must be comprehensive Internal audit must be objective Internal audit must be documented Internal audit must be followed up Internal Audit Procedures  Internal audit requires following specific procedures.
