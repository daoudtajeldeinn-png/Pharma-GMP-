# IPQC Module 4.1 Liquid Manufacturing

## Overview

Liquid manufacturing involves specific processes where IPQC is critical for quality.

## Manufacturing Process

- Solution preparation
- Mixing and homogenization
- Filtration and clarification
- Filling and sealing
- Inspection and packaging

## IPQC Control Points

- Raw material quality
- Solution clarity
- pH adjustment
- Viscosity control
- Filtration integrity

## Critical Parameters

- Temperature control
- Mixing time and speed
- pH levels
- Viscosity measurements

## Quality Considerations

- Solution stability
- Container compatibility
- Seal integrity
