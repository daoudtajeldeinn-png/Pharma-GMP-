## Overview

Risk management is essential to identify, evaluate, and control risks associated with products and processes.

What is Risk Management?

Risk management is an organized process to identify, evaluate, and control risks.

Identifying potential risks Evaluating probability and severity Controlling risks Monitoring control effectiveness of Risk Management: Protecting patients Protecting the company Regulatory compliance Continuous improvement Risk management is an integral part of GMP Risk management must be continuous Risk management requires participation from all employees Risk Management Principles  Risk management must follow specific principles.

- **Scientific Evaluation**: Using scientific methods Transparency: Open communication Participation: Involving all stakeholders Continuous Improvement: Continuous updates Proportionality: Control proportional to risks Scientific evaluation ensures accuracy Transparency builds trust Participation ensures comprehensive perspectives Continuous improvement ensures relevance Proportionality ensures efficiency Risk Management Procedures  Risk management includes several organized steps.
- **Steps**: Risk Identification: Identify all potential risks Use tools like Brainstorming Review historical data Consult experts Risk Assessment: Evaluate probability of risks occurring Evaluate severity of risks Determine risk priority Use risk matrix Risk Control: Develop control procedures Implement procedures Evaluate procedure effectiveness Update control procedures Risk Monitoring: Monitor control effectiveness Monitor new risks Update risk assessment Continuous communication Risk identification must be comprehensive Risk assessment must be objective Risk control must be effective Risk monitoring must be continuous Risk Management Tools  There are several tools that can be used for risk management.
