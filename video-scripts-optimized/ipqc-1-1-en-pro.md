# IPQC Module 1.1 Definition and Importance

## Overview

In-Process Quality Control is critical for pharmaceutical manufacturing quality.

## Key Concepts

- IPQC is performed during manufacturing
- It monitors quality attributes at each stage
- Prevents defects before final product completion
- Ensures compliance with GMP requirements

## Benefits

- Early detection of quality issues
- Reduced waste and rework
- Consistent product quality
- Regulatory compliance assurance

## Implementation

- Establish control points in manufacturing process
- Define acceptance criteria for each stage
- Train personnel on testing procedures
- Document all IPQC activities properly
