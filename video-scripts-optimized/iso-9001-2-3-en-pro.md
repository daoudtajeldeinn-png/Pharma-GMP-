## Overview

Defining the scope is essential to correctly apply the standard.

What is Quality Management System Scope?

The quality management system scope defines what the system includes and what it does not include.

Internal and external boundaries and limits Products and services included Processes included Locations included of Defining Scope: Defines what the standard applies to Defines what the standard does not apply to Facilitates application Facilitates auditing Scope must be clear Scope must be realistic Scope must be documented Scope must be reviewed Elements of Defining Scope  Defining scope includes several elements.

- **Main Elements**: Products and Services: Products included Services included Products not included Services not included Processes: Processes included Processes not included Critical processes Support processes Locations: Locations included Locations not included Main locations Subsidiary locations Departments: Departments included Departments not included Critical departments Support departments Exclusions  ISO 9001 allows certain exclusions.
- **Permitted Exclusions**: Requirements that do not apply to the nature of the organization Requirements that do not apply to the system scope Requirements that do not affect the organization's ability to achieve compliance Conditions for Exclusions: Must be justified Must be documented Must be approved Must not affect quality Exclusions must be limited Exclusions must be clear Exclusions must be reviewed Exclusions must be updated Documenting Scope  Documenting scope is essential for compliance.
