# ISO 9001 - Unit 1 History of ISO 9001

> **Pharmaceutical Quality & Compliance Training Script**

## Overview

Welcome  to this lecture.

Today we will discuss the history of ISO 9001 and its evolution over the years.

Understanding the history is essential to understanding the current standards.

Beginnings of ISO 9001  ISO 9001 has a rich history dating back to the mid-20th century.

ISO (International Organization for Standardization) was founded in 1947 ISO 9001 was first issued in 1987 It was a response to the need for international quality standards It evolved over the years to adapt to changes Before ISO 9001, each country had its own standards This created difficulties in international trade The need for unified standards became clear ISO 9001 was the solution ISO 9001 Editions  ISO 9001 went through several editions, each bringing improvements.

- **Main Editions**: ISO 9001:1987: First edition Focused on quality control 3 separate standards (9001, 9002, 9003) Based on British standards BS 5750 ISO 9001:1994: Second edition Focused on quality assurance Maintained basic structure Added additional requirements ISO 9001:2000: Third edition - major change Focused on quality management Merged the three standards into one Adopted process approach ISO 9001:2008: Fourth edition Minor improvements Additional clarifications Improved compatibility with ISO 14001 ISO 9001:2015: Fifth edition - latest edition Focused on risk management Adopted PDCA approach Major structural improvements Major Changes in 2015  The 2015 edition brought significant changes.
- **Major Changes**: High Level Structure: Unified structure with other ISO standards 10 main clauses Easy to understand and apply Facilitates integration with other standards Risk Management: Focus on risk management Risk assessment required Risk management part of all processes
