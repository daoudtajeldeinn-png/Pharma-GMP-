## Overview

Validation is essential to ensure that processes, equipment, and systems operate correctly and consistently.

What is Validation?

Validation is the process of demonstrating that any process, system, or activity consistently produces the required results.

Demonstrating with evidence that the process produces required results Ensuring consistency and quality Regulatory compliance Risk reduction Types of Validation: Equipment Validation: Proving equipment operates correctly Process Validation: Proving the process produces good product Cleaning Validation: Proving cleaning is effective Analytical Method Validation: Proving analytical methods are accurate Validation requires scientific evidence Validation must be documented Validation must be reviewed regularly Validation Principles  Validation must follow specific principles.
