## Preventive Actions

- Actions to prevent future problems
- Organized system for problem management
- Essential for regulatory compliance of CAPA:
- Prevents problem recurrence
- Improves quality
- Ensures regulatory compliance
- Reduces costs
- CAPA is an essential part of GMP
- CAPA is required by all regulatory authorities
- CAPA is essential for patient safety

## Preventive Actions

It is important to understand the difference between corrective and preventive actions.


## Corrective Actions

- Taken after the problem occurs
- Aim to correct the current problem
- Aim to prevent problem recurrence

## Preventive Actions

- Taken before the problem occurs
- Aim to prevent potential problems
- Executed based on risk analysis
- Executed based on historical data

## Corrective Actions

are a reaction to problems


## Preventive Actions

are proactive
