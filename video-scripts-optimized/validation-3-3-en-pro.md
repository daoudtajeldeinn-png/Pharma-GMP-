# Validation - Unit 3 PQ (Performance Qualification)  In this lecture, we will discuss PQ or Performance Qualification.

> **Pharmaceutical Quality & Compliance Training Script**

## Overview

PQ is the third and final stage of validation and is essential to ensure that equipment performs as required under actual operating conditions.

What is PQ?

PQ is the verification of equipment performance under actual operating conditions.

Performance qualification Ensure equipment performs as required Ensure consistency and stability Ensure product quality of PQ:

- Ensures product quality
- Reduces deviations
- Ensures patient safety
- Essential for commercial production PQ must be performed after OQ PQ must be executed under actual operating conditions PQ requires execution of multiple batches PQ Stages  PQ includes several organized stages.
- **PQ Stage 1**: Process design and development Identify parameters Define acceptance limits Develop control procedures PQ Stage 2: Process qualification Execute 3 successful batches Verify consistency Verify quality PQ Stage 3: Continued process qualification Monitor performance Monitor quality Continuous improvement for Each Stage: PQ Stage 1: Process design and development Identify critical parameters Identify critical control points Develop control strategy PQ Stage 2: Execute 3 successful batches Verify consistency Verify quality Verify compliance PQ Stage 3: Continuous performance monitoring Continuous quality monitoring Continuous improvement Continuous maintenance PQ Contents  PQ includes several elements that must be verified.
- **Main Elements**: Batch Execution: Execute 3 successful batches Execute batches according to formula Monitor all parameters Record all data Quality Testing: Test final product Test critical parameters Test critical control points Compare results with criteria Data Analysis: Analyze batch data Compare batches with each other Verify consistency Verify stability Performance Evaluation: Evaluate equipment performance Evaluate process performance Evaluate product quality Evaluate compliance Documentation: Document all activities Prepare PQ report Review report Approve report PQ Procedures  Executing PQ requires following specific procedures.
