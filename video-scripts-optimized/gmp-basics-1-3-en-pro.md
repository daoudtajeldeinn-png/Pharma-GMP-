# GMP Basics - Unit 3 Differences Between GMP, GCP, and GLP  In this lecture, we will learn about the differences between GMP, GCP, and GLP.

> **Pharmaceutical Quality & Compliance Training Script**

## Overview

These three practices together form the quality framework in the pharmaceutical industry, but each focuses on a different stage.

GMP - Good Manufacturing Practice  GMP, as we learned, focuses on the manufacturing stage.

- **Scope**: Drug manufacturing Quality control during manufacturing Storage and distribution Facility and equipment management Objective: Ensure product quality consistency Prevent contamination and errors Ensure regulatory compliance Examples: Manufacturing tablets and capsules Filling liquid medications Storing raw materials and finished products GCP - Good Clinical Practice  GCP focuses on the clinical trial stage.
- **Scope**: Designing clinical trials Conducting clinical trials Collecting and analyzing data Protecting participants' rights Objective: Ensure participant safety in trials Collect reliable data Protect human rights Examples: Phase I trials Phase II/III trials Bioavailability studies GCP includes: Ethical approval Participant selection Trial monitoring Report preparation GLP - Good Laboratory Practice  GLP focuses on the preclinical research and development stage.
- **Scope**: Preclinical studies Toxicity testing Efficacy testing Sample analysis Objective: Ensure preclinical data quality Minimize laboratory errors Ensure result consistency Examples: Toxicity testing on animals Stability studies Efficacy testing in laboratory GLP includes: Laboratory management Employee training Equipment maintenance Document management Relationship Between the Three  GMP, GCP, and GLP complement each other.
