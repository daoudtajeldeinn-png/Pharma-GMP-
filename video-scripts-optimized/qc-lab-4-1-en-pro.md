## Overview

Safety is essential to protect employees and the laboratory.

of Laboratory Safety  Laboratory safety is essential for several vital reasons.

- **Main Reasons**: Employee Protection: Protect employees from injuries Laboratory Protection: Protect laboratory from damage Compliance: Compliance with safety regulations Efficiency: Efficiency in work Trust: Employee trust in the laboratory Safety first Prevention is better than cure Every employee is responsible for safety Continuous training is essential Laboratory Hazards  There are several hazards in the laboratory that must be addressed.
- **Main Hazards**: Chemical Hazards: Hazardous chemicals Toxic chemicals Corrosive chemicals Explosive chemicals Biological Hazards: Bacteria Fungi Viruses Other microorganisms Physical Hazards: Heat Cold Radiation Electricity Mechanical Hazards: Moving equipment Sharp tools Broken glass Falls Fire Hazards: Fires Explosions Spills Gases Safety Procedures  Safety procedures are essential to prevent accidents.
Main Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Chemical-resistant lab coats Gloves Safety glasses Masks Ventilation: Good laboratory ventilation Safety cabinets Fume hoods Air quality monitoring Hazardous Material Storage: Store hazardous materials correctly Store hazardous materials separately Store hazardous materials by type Store hazardous materials by sensitivity Emergency Fire procedures Spill procedures Injury procedures Evacuation procedures Training: Safety training Emergency procedure training Equipment use training Hazardous material handling training Incident Management  Incident management is essential to handle accidents when they occur.
