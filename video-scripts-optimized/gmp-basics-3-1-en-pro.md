## Overview

Well-trained personnel are the foundation of GMP and guarantee product quality.

of Personnel in GMP  Personnel are the most important element in the GMP system.

Even the best equipment and facilities cannot ensure quality without trained personnel.

Why Are Personnel Important?

They execute the processes They discover problems They document activities They maintain quality Every employee must be trained Training must be continuous Competence must be evaluated regularly Records must be kept Types of Training  There are several types of training, each serving a different purpose.

- **Training Record Contents**: Employee name Training date Training topic Trainer name Training duration Evaluation results Employee and trainer signatures Record Management: Store records centrally Easy access to records Keep records for a specified period Protect records from damage Records must be up-to-date Records must be accurate Records must be traceable Records must be available for inspection Competence Evaluation  Competence evaluation is essential to ensure employees are capable of correctly performing their tasks.
