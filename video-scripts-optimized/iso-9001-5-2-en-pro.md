## Overview

Competence is essential for the success of the quality management system.

What is Competence?

Competence is the ability to execute tasks correctly and effectively.

Required knowledge Required skills Required experience Ability to apply of Competence:

- Ensures work quality
- Reduces errors Increases efficiency Increases effectiveness Competence must be appropriate for the role Competence must be current Competence must be evaluated Competence must be documented Types of Competence  There are several types of competence that must be developed.
- **Technical Competence**: Technical knowledge Technical skills Technical experience Technical application ability Management Competence: Management knowledge Management skills Management experience Management ability Operational Competence: Operational knowledge Operational skills Operational experience Operational ability Quality Competence: Quality knowledge Quality skills Quality experience Quality management ability Organizational Competence: Organizational knowledge Organizational skills Organizational experience Organizational ability Developing Competence  Developing competence requires following specific procedures.
Identify Competence Needs: Identify required competence Identify current competence Identify gaps Identify priorities Develop Training Plan: Define required training Define training methods Define timeline Define resources Execute Training: Execute training according to plan Monitor training Evaluate training Document Apply Competence: Apply competence in work Monitor application Evaluate application Improve application Evaluate Competence: Evaluate competence after training Evaluate training effectiveness Identify improvement areas Update training plan Evaluating Competence  Evaluating competence is essential to ensure effectiveness.
