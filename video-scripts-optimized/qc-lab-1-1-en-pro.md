What is QC Lab?

QC Lab is the quality control laboratory responsible for inspecting and testing materials and products.

Quality control laboratory Responsible for inspecting raw materials Responsible for inspecting final products Responsible for quality monitoring Main Functions: Inspect raw materials Inspect products during manufacturing Inspect final products Monitor stability Microbiological testing of QC Lab:

- Ensures quality of raw materials
- Ensures quality of products
- Ensures patient safety
- Essential for regulatory compliance ISO/IEC 17025  ISO/IEC 17025 is the international standard for laboratories.
International standard for laboratories Defines competence requirements Defines quality requirements

- Essential for accreditation of ISO/IEC 17025:
- Ensures laboratory competence
- Ensures quality of results
- Essential for accreditation
- Essential for trust Main Requirements: Quality management Personnel Facilities and equipment Analytical methods Document management Role of QC Lab in Pharmaceutical Industry  QC Lab has a vital role in the pharmaceutical industry.
