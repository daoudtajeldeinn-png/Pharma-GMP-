# IPQC Module 2.1: Tablet Manufacturing

## Overview

Tablet manufacturing involves multiple stages where In-Process Quality Control is critical to ensure product quality and consistency.

## Manufacturing Stages

- Weighing and dispensing
- Granulation and drying
- Blending and mixing
- Compression and tableting
- Coating and polishing
- Packaging

## IPQC Control Points

- Raw material quality
- Granule properties
- Blend uniformity
- Tablet hardness
- Weight variation
- Disintegration time

## Critical Parameters

- Moisture content
- Particle size distribution
- Bulk density
- Flow properties
- Compression force
- Tablet thickness

## Quality Assurance

- Process validation
- Equipment qualification
- Environmental controls
- Personnel training
- Documentation standards
