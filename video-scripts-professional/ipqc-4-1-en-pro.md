# IPQC Module 4.1: Liquid Manufacturing

## Overview

Liquid manufacturing involves specific processes where In-Process Quality Control is critical to ensure product quality and stability.

## Manufacturing Process

- Solution preparation
- Mixing and homogenization
- Filtration and clarification
- Filling and sealing
- Inspection and packaging
- Sterilization (if required)

## IPQC Control Points

- Raw material quality
- Solution clarity
- pH adjustment
- Viscosity control
- Filtration integrity
- Fill volume accuracy

## Critical Parameters

- Temperature control
- Mixing time and speed
- pH levels
- Viscosity measurements
- Filtration efficiency
- Fill accuracy

## Quality Considerations

- Solution stability
- Container compatibility
- Seal integrity
- Sterility assurance
- Preservative effectiveness
